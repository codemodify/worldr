## Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: original high-fidelity product vision image for the worldr Linux spatial workspace, wide 16:9 landscape.
Primary request: a breathtaking, cinematic computing environment inspired by the engineering workspaces in Iron Man and Avatar. The user's real ambition is a daily workspace for engineers, researchers, and ordinary people, with existing browsers and terminals sharing navigable depth with native 3D tools.
Composition: an edge-to-edge desktop interface as seen on an ordinary monitor; no monitor bezel, physical room, people, hands, headset, or film characters. Show a convincing continuous spatial canvas with three clear depth planes. A detailed native turbine assembly occupies the central foreground, partially exploded, with elegant cyan edge illumination and a few precise object-attached annotations. In the middle distance, a Chromium-style browser window displays a restrained research page with a figure and short paragraphs. A small group of two ordinary terminal windows sits farther back and to the side, partly occluded by the foreground assembly and its instrument panel. Perspective and overlapping silhouettes must make forward/backward placement immediately understandable. Keep active content nearly face-on and clearly readable; modest angles only on background panels.
Visual language: dramatic dark graphite and deep blue space, luminous cyan/ice-blue structure, restrained warm amber selection accents, finely layered glass borders, matte dark content backgrounds behind text, subtle atmospheric depth, exquisite lighting on the model, precise typography, purposeful technical detail, generous breathing room. High cinematic impact with believable information hierarchy. Original product identity, not a replica of a movie frame. Rich finish without an indiscriminate neon grid or wall of meaningless rings.
UI: small worldr wordmark and project breadcrumb across the top, fine perimeter chrome, a compact workspace overview along the bottom with a few spatial positions, a clear selected-object outline, a small connected response chart marked illustrative, visible focus/overview controls. Browser and terminal interiors should look like ordinary desktop applications embedded in this new spatial environment. A native tool can visually surround a 3D object. Only a few short labels, no dense invented readouts or fake performance statistics.
Text to render accurately: "worldr"; "PROPULSION / WORKSPACE 01"; "AXIAL STUDY"; "Illustrative model"; "Research"; "Terminal"; "Focus"; "Overview". Terminal may contain short ordinary commands such as "git status" and "nvim flow.py".
Disclosure: put a small but readable footer along the bottom edge saying exactly "DESIGN CONCEPT — NOT A RUNNING BUILD".
Overall: this should look like an ambitious, meticulously designed future operating environment with real useful applications, spatial organization, and native scientific objects. Deliver one coherent view, not a collage, presentation board, or split-screen comparison.
